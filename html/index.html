Route lookups send the callsign and position of visible aircraft to the route service () and show its result. Routes come from schedules and can be wrong; a route that doesn't fit the aircraft's position is marked as unconfirmed.

Airline logos are loaded from . They belong to their owners, and loading them shares your IP address with that service. With logos off (or when a logo isn't available) a circle with the airline's three-letter code is shown instead, and nothing is requested.
